google_adsense: true
---

**If you already have a WurstForum account, you can skip to step 2.**

1. Go to [WurstForum.net](https://wurstforum.net/) and click <kbd>Sign Up</kbd> at the top to create an account.

   <img src="https://images.wurstclient.net/_media/tutorials/snapshot_notifications/wurstforum_sign_up.webp" alt="screenshot of the 'Sign Up' dialog on WurstForum.net" width="373" height="527" />

2. Go to the [Snapshot Announcements](https://wurstforum.net/t/announcements-snapshots) section on WurstForum.

   <img src="https://images.wurstclient.net/_media/tutorials/snapshot_notifications/wurstforum_snapshot_announcements_section.webp" alt="screenshot of the 'Snapshot Announcements' section on WurstForum.net" width="1024" height="768" />

3. Click the <kbd>⭐ Follow</kbd> button at the top-left of the page.

4. In the dialog that appears, change your subscription level to <kbd>Lurking</kbd> and confirm.

   <img src="https://images.wurstclient.net/_media/tutorials/snapshot_notifications/wurstforum_lurking.webp" alt="screenshot of the 'Follow' dialog on WurstForum.net with the 'Lurking' subscription setting selected" width="596" height="461" />

_Happy snapshot hacking!_
