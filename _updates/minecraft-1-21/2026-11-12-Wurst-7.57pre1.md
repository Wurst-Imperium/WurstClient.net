## Changelog

- Improved Vulcan and NoCheat+ bypasses of AimAssist, KillauraLegit and TriggerBot.

- Added an "Aim at" setting to KillauraLegit (same as in AimAssist).

- Added an "Ignore mouse input" slider to KillauraLegit (same as in AimAssist).

- AimAssist and KillauraLegit now aim much more smoothly and much more precisely.

- AutoSword now selects a weapon as soon as Killaura finds a target and keeps it selected between attacks.

- Killaura's damage indicator now updates more quickly when the target changes between attacks.

- AutoFish+KillauraLegit now disable each other, just like AutoFish+AimAssist do, to avoid them fighting over where to aim.

- KillauraLegit no longer tries to attack while you are blocking with a shield (impossible in vanilla, not legit) but keeps aiming at the target during that time.

- AimAssist's "Aim while blocking" setting is now enabled by default.

- "Aim at: Auto" and "Aim at: Feet" no longer aim so close to the edge of the target's hitbox, resulting in fewer missed attacks.

- Fixed AimAssist and KillauraLegit aiming more slowly than the "Rotation speed" setting would suggest.

- Fixed AimAssist and KillauraLegit aiming very slowly when using a low mouse sensitivity setting. ([#1001](https://github.com/Wurst-Imperium/Wurst7/issues/1001), [#1141](https://github.com/Wurst-Imperium/Wurst7/issues/1141), [/d/667](https://wurstforum.net/d/667), [/d/783](https://wurstforum.net/d/783))

- **Known issue:** AimAssist and KillauraLegit currently resist manual mouse movements much more strongly than they used to, even at 0% "Ignore mouse input".

- Fixed Killaura dealing very little damage on its first attack after AutoSword switches weapons when using "Speed: auto".

- Fixed Killaura attacking slightly later than necessary when using "Speed: auto".

- Fixed Killaura's damage indicator staying visible while paused on containers.

- Fixed all FOV sliders in Wurst doing their math wrong, resulting in increasingly wrong angles as you get closer to looking straight up/down.

- All FOV sliders now have the tooltip explaining that it stands for Field Of View.

- Fixed some edge cases where AimAssist could get stuck wobbling its aim back and forth between two positions that both miss the target's hitbox.
