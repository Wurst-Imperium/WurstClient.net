## Changelog

- Fixed X-Ray no longer working correctly when Sodium is installed. ([#1241](https://github.com/Wurst-Imperium/Wurst7/issues/1241), [/d/1375](https://wurstforum.net/d/1375), [/d/1381](https://wurstforum.net/d/1381), [/d/1391](https://wurstforum.net/d/1391))

- Fixed AutoFarm being unable to harvest chorus flowers that have a block placed above them. ([/d/1377](https://wurstforum.net/d/1377))

- Migrated all of Wurst's automated tests to the Fabric Client GameTest API. (Minecraft 1.21.4 and later versions only)

- Migrated from Yarn to official mappings to prepare for the [removal of obfuscation](https://fabricmc.net/2025/10/31/obfuscation.html) in Minecraft 1.21.11.

- Added an automated Sodium compatibility test. (Minecraft 1.21.4 and later versions only)

- Fixed NoShieldOverlay not doing anything while you are blocking with a shield in Minecraft 1.21.4 or 1.21.8. ([#1240](https://github.com/Wurst-Imperium/Wurst7/issues/1240))

See also: [Wurst 7.51 changelog](/updates/wurst-7-51/)
