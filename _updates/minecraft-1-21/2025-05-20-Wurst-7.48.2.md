## Changelog

- Fixed X-Ray not showing fluids correctly. ([/d/1139](https://wurstforum.net/d/1139))

- Added `minecraft:wall_torch` to X-Ray's default list of ores.

- Added water and lava to the automated X-Ray test.

- Fixed a crash when pressing the <kbd>del</kbd> key while editing a BlockList (e.g. X-Ray ores) without first selecting a list entry to be deleted.

- Fixed a crash when using AutoSword while having shears in the hotbar.

- Fixed all(?) remaining rendering issues in the 1.21.6 snapshots (as of 25w20a).
