## Changelog

- Fixed a compatibility issue with Sodium 0.6.1. Sodium 0.6.0 remains supported too. ([#1088](https://github.com/Wurst-Imperium/Wurst7/issues/1088))

- Dropped support for Sodium 0.4 and 0.5, which don't actually exist for modern Minecraft versions anymore.
