## Changelog

- Added NoVignette: Removes the vignette effect (darkening of the screen edges). ([#1152](https://github.com/Wurst-Imperium/Wurst7/pull/1152))

- NoOverlay now also removes the overlay of powder snow. ([#1153](https://github.com/Wurst-Imperium/Wurst7/pull/1153))

- Added a "Pause when sneaking" option to Glide (enabled by default). ([#919](https://github.com/Wurst-Imperium/Wurst7/pull/919))

- Added a "Logout" button to AltManager: Restores the session you got from the Minecraft launcher. Useful if you don't have your main account in AltManager. ([#1143](https://github.com/Wurst-Imperium/Wurst7/pull/1143))

- Fixed the skin preview in AltManager only displaying the offline-mode skins of your accounts. (Minecraft 1.21.1 and later versions only) ([#154](https://github.com/Wurst-Imperium/Wurst7/issues/154))

- Fixed the "Last Server" button flashing on for a split-second when you open the Multiplayer menu and don't have a last server.

- Fixed several buttons on the AltManager and ServerFinder screens flashing on for a split-second when you open them. ([#1146](https://github.com/Wurst-Imperium/Wurst7/pull/1146))

- Fixed the ServerFinder and CleanUp screens not taking you back to the multiplayer screen when you press Escape.

- AltManager, ServerFinder, and CleanUp screens now support pressing Mouse4 (the "back" button) to go back to the previous screen.

- Fixed NoFog removing more types of fog than it is supposed to, creating a sharp line in the sky. ([#1151](https://github.com/Wurst-Imperium/Wurst7/pull/1151), [#1156](https://github.com/Wurst-Imperium/Wurst7/pull/1156))

- Fixed the fake players created by Blink, Freecam, and RemoteView being able to push other entities away (most noticeable when using Blink). ([#1155](https://github.com/Wurst-Imperium/Wurst7/pull/1155))

- A copy of the GPL 3.0 license is now included in the JAR file. ([#1142](https://github.com/Wurst-Imperium/Wurst7/issues/1142))
